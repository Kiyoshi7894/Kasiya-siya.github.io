RELYENONG BANGUS
PANGASINAN/ DAGUPAN CUISINE
• RELYENONG BANGUS IS A WELL-KNOWN FILIPINO STUFFED MILKFISH DISH ASSOCIATED WITH PANGASINAN AND DAGUPAN, AREAS RECOGNIZED FOR THEIR BANGUS PRODUCTION. THE FISH MEAT IS CAREFULLY SEPARATED FROM ITS SKIN, DEBONED, AND MIXED WITH VEGETABLES AND SEASONINGS. THE MIXTURE IS THEN PLACED BACK INSIDE THE FISH SKIN AND FRIED OR BAKED UNTIL GOLDEN BROWN. IT IS OFTEN PREPARED FOR SPECIAL OCCASIONS AND FAMILY CELEBRATIONS.
INGREDIENTS

• RELYENONG BANGUS INGREDIENTS

  • 1 LARGE BANGUS (MILKFISH), CLEANED
  • 1 SMALL CARROT, FINELY CHOPPED
  • 1 SMALL ONION, CHOPPED
  • 3 CLOVES GARLIC, MINCED
  • 1 SMALL RED BELL PEPPER, CHOPPED
  • ½ CUP GREEN PEAS
  • 1 EGG, BEATEN
  • 1 TABLESPOON SOY SAUCE
  • SALT AND PEPPER TO TASTE
  • COOKING OIL
  • CALAMANSI JUICE (OPTIONAL)
STEP BY STEP
  • CAREFULLY SEPARATE THE FISH MEAT FROM THE SKIN WHILE KEEPING THE SKIN INTACT.
  • REMOVE THE BONES FROM THE FISH MEAT AND FLAKE IT INTO SMALL PIECES.
  • HEAT COOKING OIL IN A PAN.
  • SAUTÉ THE GARLIC AND ONION UNTIL FRAGRANT.
  • ADD THE CARROT, BELL PEPPER, AND GREEN PEAS.
  • ADD THE FLAKED FISH MEAT AND SOY SAUCE.
  • SEASON WITH SALT AND PEPPER, THEN COOK UNTIL WELL COMBINED.
  • ALLOW THE MIXTURE TO COOL BEFORE MIXING IN THE BEATEN EGG.
  • CAREFULLY STUFF THE MIXTURE BACK INTO THE FISH SKIN.
  • SECURE THE OPENING WITH FOOD-SAFE SKEWERS OR KITCHEN STRING.
  • FRY OR BAKE UNTIL GOLDEN BROWN AND FULLY COOKED.
  • ALLOW IT TO COOL SLIGHTLY BEFORE SLICING AND SERVING.
