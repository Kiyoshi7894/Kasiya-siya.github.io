TUPIG
ILOCOS REGION (REGION I) & CENTRAL LUZON (REGION III - TARLAC), PHILIPPINES
• TUPIG IS A TRADITIONAL FILIPINO RICE CAKE DELICACY POPULAR ACROSS THE ILOCOS REGION (REGION I) AND CENTRAL LUZON (PARTICULARLY PROVINCES LIKE TARLAC). IT IS MADE FROM A CHEWY BLEND OF GLUTINOUS RICE FLOUR (GALAPONG), GRATED YOUNG COCONUT, SUGAR, AND COCONUT MILK, WRAPPED SNUGLY IN WILTED BANANA LEAVES AND GRILLED OVER HOT CHARCOALS UNTIL SMOKY, FRAGRANT, AND CHARRED.
INGREDIENTS

• TUPIG INGREDIENTS

  • 2 CUPS GLUTINOUS RICE FLOUR (GALAPONG)
  • 1 CUP GRATED YOUNG COCONUT (BUKO)
  • ¾ CUP BROWN SUGAR
  • 1 CUP COCONUT MILK (GATA)
  • ½ CUP YOUNG COCONUT STRIPS (MACAPUNO OR *BUKO* STRIPS, OPTIONAL)
  • 2 TABLESPOONS MELTED BUTTER OR MARGARINE
  • BANANA LEAVES, CUT INTO RECTANGLES AND PASSED OVER FLAME TO WILT
STEP BY STEP
  • IN A LARGE MIXING BOWL, COMBINE GLUTINOUS RICE FLOUR, BROWN SUGAR, AND GRATED YOUNG COCONUT.
  • GRADUALLY POUR IN THE COCONUT MILK AND MELTED BUTTER, STIRRING CONTINUOUSLY UNTIL A SMOOTH, THICK, AND UNIFORM BATTER FORMS.
  • FOLD IN THE YOUNG COCONUT STRIPS IF USING FOR ADDED TEXTURE.
  • SPOON ABOUT 2 TO 3 TABLESPOONS OF THE MIXTURE ONTO THE CENTER OF EACH PREPARED, WILTED BANANA LEAF.
  • ROLL THE BANANA LEAF SECURELY AROUND THE MIXTURE AND FOLD OR TUCK BOTH ENDS TO SEAL THE WRAPPER.
  • GRILL THE WRAPPED TUPIG OVER HOT CHARCOALS FOR ABOUT 5 TO 7 MINUTES PER SIDE, TURNING FREQUENTLY UNTIL THE BANANA LEAVES ARE CHARRED AND THE RICE CAKE INSIDE IS COOKED THROUGH AND CHEWY.
  • LET IT COOL SLIGHTLY, UNWRAP FROM THE BANANA LEAVES, AND SERVE WARM.
