LECHE FLAN
Philippines
- LECHE FLAN IS A BELOVED FILIPINO CARAMEL CUSTARD DESSERT MADE FROM EGG YOLKS, CONDENSED MILK, AND EVAPORATED MILK, TOPPED WITH A RICH GOLDEN CARAMEL SYRUP. IT IS FAMOUS FOR ITS ULTRA-CREAMY, SMOOTH TEXTURE AND IS A STAPLE SWEET TREAT AT EVERY SPECIAL OCCASION AND HOLIDAY GATHERING.
INGREDIENTS

• LECHE FLAN INGREDIENTS

  • 10 LARGE EGG YOLKS
  • 1 CAN (14 OZ) SWEETENED CONDENSED MILK
  • 1 CUP EVAPORATED MILK
  • 1 TEASPOON VANILLA EXTRACT
  • 1 CUP GRANULATED SUGAR (FOR CARAMEL)
  • ¼ CUP WATER (FOR CARAMEL)
STEP BY STEP
  • PREPARE THE CARAMEL SYRUP BY COMBINING SUGAR AND WATER IN A METAL MOLD (LIPERSA). HEAT OVER LOW FLAME UNTIL THE SUGAR MELTS AND TURNS INTO A GOLDEN-BROWN LIQUID. LET IT COOL AND SET.
  • IN A LARGE BOWL, GENTLY WHISK THE EGG YOLKS WITHOUT INCORPORATING TOO MANY AIR BUBBLES.
  • SLOWLY STIR IN THE CONDENSED MILK, EVAPORATED MILK, AND VANILLA EXTRACT UNTIL COMPLETELY COMBINED.
  • STRAIN THE CUSTARD MIXTURE THROUGH A FINE-MESH SIEVE AT LEAST TWICE TO ENSURE A SILKY SMOOTH TEXTURE.
  • POUR THE STRAINED CUSTARD INTO THE PREPARED MOLDS OVER THE SET CARAMEL.
  • COVER EACH MOLD TIGHTLY WITH ALUMINUM FOIL.
  • STEAM OVER LOW HEAT (WATER BATH OR BAIN-MARIE) FOR 30 TO 45 MINUTES, OR BAKE IN A WATER BATH AT 350°F (175°C) UNTIL FIRM.
  • LET IT COOL COMPLETELY, THEN CHILL IN THE REFRIGERATOR FOR AT LEAST 3 HOURS BEFORE UNMOLDING AND SERVING.
