BICOL EXPRESS
Bicolano-Inspired Filipino Cuisine
• BICOL EXPRESS IS A CLASSIC FILIPINO STEW MADE WITH TENDER BITES OF PORK BELLY SIMMERED IN COCONUT MILK (GATA) AND COCONUT CREAM (KAKANG GATA), HEAVILY SEASONED WITH FERMENTED SHRIMP PASTE (BAGOONG ALAMANG), GARLIC, ONIONS, AND GENEROUS AMOUNTS OF CHILI PEPPERS. KNOWN FOR ITS DISTINCT BALANCE OF RICH COCONUT CREAMINESS, DEEP UMAMI SALTINESS, AND FIERY HEAT, IT PAYS HOMAGE TO THE CHILI-AND-COCONUT-CENTRIC CULINARY TRADITION OF THE BICOL REGION.
INGREDIENTS

• BICOL EXPRESS INGREDIENTS

  • 2 LBS (APPROX. 900G) PORK BELLY, CUT INTO BITE-SIZED CUBES
  • 1 ONION
  • 6 CLOVES GARLIC, MINCED
  • 1 GINGER, MINCED
  • 2 CUPS COCONUT MILK (GATA)
  • 1 CUP COCONUT CREAM (KAKANG GATA)
  • 3 TO 4 TBSP FERMENTED SHRIMP PASTE (BAGOONG ALAMANG)
  • 6 TO 8 FINGER CHILIES (SILING HABA)
  • 3 TO 5 (SILING LABUYO / RED CHILIES), CHOPPED
  • 1 TBSP COOKING OIL
  • SALT AND GROUND BLACK PEPPER TO TASTE
ALTERNATIVE INGREDIENTS

• BUDGET BICOL EXPRESS INGREDIENTS

  • 300G PORK MASK / MINGGAY OR KASIM CUTS
  • 3 BLOCKS HARD TOFU (TOKWA), DICED & FRIED
  • 1 CAN (400ML) COCONUT MILK (GATA)
  • 2 TABLESPOONS SAUTÉED ALAMANG (SHRIMP PASTE)
  • 6-8 PCS GREEN CHILI (SILING HABA), SLICED
  • 3-5 PCS RED CHILI (SILING LABUYO), CHOPPED
  • 1 MEDIUM RED ONION & 4 CLOVES GARLIC, MINCED
  • 1 THUMB-SIZED GINGER, MINCED
  • 1 TABLESPOON COOKING OIL
STEP BY STEP
  • SLICE GREEN FINGER CHILIES AND RED BIRD'S EYE CHILIES, DESEEDING THE GREEN ONES IF YOU PREFER LESS HEAT.
  • HEAT OIL IN A WOK, ADD PORK BELLY CUBES, AND RENDER UNTIL LIGHT GOLDEN BROWN.
  • PUSH PORK ASIDE, SAUTÉ GARLIC, ONION, AND GINGER UNTIL SOFT, THEN STIR-FRY SHRIMP PASTE UNTIL FRAGRANT.
  • POUR IN COCONUT MILK, COVER, AND SIMMER FOR 20 TO 25 MINUTES UNTIL THE PORK IS TENDER.
  • STIR IN SLICED FINGER CHILIES AND BIRD'S EYE CHILIES TO DISTRIBUTE HEAT THROUGHOUT THE SAUCE.
  • THICKEN WITH COCONUT CREAM, POUR IN COCONUT CREAM AND SIMMER UNCOVERED OVER LOW HEAT UNTIL THE SAUCE THICKENS AND RENDERS OIL.
  • ADJUST SEASONING WITH SALT, PEPPER, OR SHRIMP PASTE TO TASTE, THEN SERVE HOT OVER STEAMED WHITE RICE.
  • SERVE HOT WITH STEAMED RICE.
