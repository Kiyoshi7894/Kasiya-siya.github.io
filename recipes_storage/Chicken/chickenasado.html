CHICKEN ASADO
KAPAMPANGAN CUISINE
• CHICKEN ASADO IS A FILIPINO CHICKEN DISH POPULAR IN KAPAMPANGAN COOKING. IT IS MADE WITH CHICKEN SIMMERED IN A SAVORY AND SLIGHTLY SWEET SAUCE WITH TOMATO SAUCE, SOY SAUCE, CALAMANSI, GARLIC, AND ONIONS. IT IS KNOWN FOR ITS RICH FLAVOR AND TENDER CHICKEN. IT IS BEST SERVED WITH STEAMED RICE.
ALTERNATIVE INGREDIENTS

• BUDGET CHICKEN & TOKWA ASADO INGREDIENTS

  • 500G CHICKEN CUTS (NECK, WINGS, OR FEET)
  • 3 BLOCKS HARD TOFU (TOKWA), CUBED & FRIED
  • 1 SMALL PACK (150G) TOMATO SAUCE
  • ¼ CUP REGULAR SOY SAUCE
  • 2 TABLESPOONS CALAMANSI JUICE
  • 2 MEDIUM POTATOES, QUARTERED
  • 1 MEDIUM RED ONION & 4 CLOVES GARLIC, MINCED
  • 2 DRIED BAY LEAVES & 1 STAR ANISE
  • 1.5 TABLESPOONS BROWN SUGAR
  • 1 TABLESPOON COOKING OIL
INGREDIENTS

• CHICKEN ASADO INGREDIENTS

  • 1 KG CHICKEN, CUT INTO SERVING PIECES
  • 1/2 CUP TOMATO SAUCE
  • 1/4 CUP SOY SAUCE
  • 1/4 CUP CALAMANSI JUICE
  • 1 MEDIUM ONION, CHOPPED
  • 5 CLOVES GARLIC, MINCED
  • 2 MEDIUM POTATOES, CUBED
  • 2 TABLESPOONS COOKING OIL
  • 1 CUP WATER OR CHICKEN BROTH
  • 2 TABLESPOONS BROWN SUGAR
  • 2 BAY LEAVES
  • 1/2 TEASPOON GROUND BLACK PEPPER
  • SALT TO TASTE
STEP BY STEP
  • 1. PREPARE THE INGREDIENTS. WASH THE CHICKEN AND CHOP THE GARLIC, ONION, AND POTATOES. SET ASIDE.
  • 2. MARINATE THE CHICKEN. IN A BOWL, COMBINE THE CHICKEN, SOY SAUCE, CALAMANSI JUICE, AND BLACK PEPPER. MIX WELL AND MARINATE FOR 30 MINUTES.
  • 3. SAUTÉ THE AROMATICS. HEAT COOKING OIL IN A PAN. ADD GARLIC AND ONION. SAUTÉ UNTIL FRAGRANT.
  • 4. ADD THE CHICKEN. PUT THE MARINATED CHICKEN INTO THE PAN. COOK UNTIL THE OUTSIDE CHANGES COLOR.
  • 5. ADD THE TOMATO SAUCE. POUR IN THE TOMATO SAUCE AND MIX WELL UNTIL THE CHICKEN IS COATED.
  • 6. ADD WATER AND SEASONINGS. POUR IN THE WATER OR CHICKEN BROTH. ADD BROWN SUGAR AND BAY LEAVES. STIR WELL.
  • 7. SIMMER THE CHICKEN. COVER THE PAN AND COOK OVER LOW HEAT UNTIL THE CHICKEN IS TENDER AND FULLY COOKED.
  • 8. ADD THE POTATOES. PUT THE POTATOES INTO THE PAN AND COOK UNTIL THEY BECOME SOFT.
  • 9. ADJUST THE TASTE. ADD SALT AND BLACK PEPPER ACCORDING TO YOUR PREFERENCE. SIMMER UNTIL THE SAUCE THICKENS.
  • 10. SERVE AND ENJOY! TRANSFER THE CHICKEN ASADO TO A SERVING PLATE. SERVE HOT WITH STEAMED RICE.
